LESSON 3 – VERBS WITH PREPOSITIONS & RELATIVE CLAUSES

Verbs with prepositions: collocations and patterns
  • In English, many verbs are followed by specific prepositions. These combinations form fixed verb collocations that cannot be changed. e.g. Many households depend on solar energy.
  • The preposition is always followed by a noun phrase, pronoun, or gerund (V-ing).
Preposition Common Verb Collocations Context Meaning Example (Unit Context)
for apologize for, blame for, search for, care for, prepare for Expresses purpose, cause, reason, or responsibility The chemical plant apologized for polluting the local river ecosystem.
to belong to, contribute to, adapt to, object to, listen to Expresses direction, recipient, or consequence Excessive plastic packaging contributes to catastrophic marine waste.
on rely on, depend on, concentrate on, insist on, base on Expresses reliance, focus, or foundation Successful green living depends on grassroots community initiatives.
in succeed in, participate in, believe in, result in, invest in Expresses involvement, success, or outcome The school community succeeded in reducing single-use plastics by 80%.
from protect from, prevent from, recover from, suffer from Expresses separation, origin, or defense Planting urban trees protects citizens from hazardous air pollution.
about worry about, care about, complain about, think about Expresses mental focus, concern, or emotion Environmentalists worry about rising global temperatures and ice melts.
⚠ WATCH OUT!
  • Preposition + Gerund Rule: A verb coming directly after a preposition MUST take the -ing form (gerund). Never use a bare infinitive or to-infinitive!
    • Correct: 'They succeeded in conserving freshwater.' (NOT: succeeded in conserve)
  • Beware of 'to' as a preposition: In verbs like contribute to, look forward to, object to, 'to' is a preposition, so it is followed by V-ing:
    • 'Everyone can contribute to protecting the planet.'
Relative clauses referring to a whole sentence (with 'which')
  • We can use a non-defining relative clause introduced by which to comment on or describe the result of an entire preceding clause, not just the single noun before it.
  • This structure creates elegant, cohesive academic writing by showing cause, effect, or evaluation in a single flowing sentence.
Structure / Pattern Communicative Purpose Example (Unit Context)
[Clause], which + verb... States a direct result or outcome of the preceding fact The city council installed solar lighting, which saved thousands of kilowatt-hours.
[Clause], which is + adjective... Provides the writer's judgment, attitude, or comment Many families have started composting kitchen waste, which is highly commendable.
[Clause], which means that... Explains the logical consequence or implication Students bring their own water bottles, which means that no plastic trash is created.
⚠ WATCH OUT!
  • You MUST always use a comma (,) before which when it refers to the entire sentence!
    • The students cleaned the park, which impressed local residents.
  • You can NEVER use 'that' or 'what' to replace 'which' in this sentence-commenting structure!
    • Wrong: The students cleaned the park, that impressed everyone.
    • Wrong: The students cleaned the park, what impressed everyone.

1
The eco-friendly supermarket focuses on (reduce) single-use plastic packaging by offering biodegradable cloth tote bags.
💡 Giải thích: Sau giới từ 'on' trong cụm 'focus on', động từ theo sau chia ở dạng danh động từ (V-ing). (Đáp án: reducing)
2
The youth volunteer club succeeded in (clean) up three tons of plastic litter along the polluted river canal.
💡 Giải thích: Cấu trúc 'succeed in doing sth': sau giới từ 'in' động từ có dạng danh động từ 'cleaning'. (Đáp án: cleaning)
3
Our school installed an automatic light in every corridor, which (save) a tremendous amount of electrical energy each month.
💡 Giải thích: Mệnh đề quan hệ ', which' thay thế cho toàn bộ sự việc ở mệnh đề trước (coi là chủ ngữ số ít), động từ chia thì hiện tại đơn ngôi thứ ba số ít ('saves'). (Đáp án: saves)
4
Instead of throwing away plastic takeaway containers, we should (reuse) every container as a plant pot.
💡 Giải thích: Sau động từ khuyết thiếu 'should', động từ giữ nguyên thể không 'to' ('reuse'). (Đáp án: reuse)
5
The chemical plant manager apologised to local residents for (fail) to prevent industrial pollutants from leaking into the reservoir.
💡 Giải thích: Cấu trúc 'apologise for doing sth': sau giới từ 'for' động từ chia ở dạng danh động từ ('failing'). (Đáp án: failing)
6
Many suburban families depend on (compost) household waste to produce rich natural fertiliser for their vegetable patches.
💡 Giải thích: Sau giới từ 'on' trong cụm 'depend on', động từ chia V-ing ('composting'). (Đáp án: composting)
7
The community planted five hundred shade trees in the public square, which (provide) fresh air and shade for neighborhood children.
💡 Giải thích: Mệnh đề quan hệ ', which' đại diện cho toàn bộ sự việc trồng cây, động từ chia ngôi thứ ba số ít 'provides'. (Đáp án: provides)
8
Transitioning to green energy and public transit directly (contribute) to reducing dangerous greenhouse gas emissions.
💡 Giải thích: Chủ ngữ là danh động từ 'Transitioning...' (ngôi thứ ba số ít), đi với 'contribute to' thì hiện tại đơn chia 'contributes'. (Đáp án: contributes)
9
Volunteers spent their entire Saturday morning (sort) recyclable cardboard boxes and glass jars at the community center.
💡 Giải thích: Cấu trúc 'spend time doing sth': chia V-ing ('sorting'). (Đáp án: sorting)
10
Non-biodegradable plastics take centuries to (decompose) in landfills, which severely damages underground soil ecosystems.
💡 Giải thích: Cấu trúc 'take + time + to-V': sau 'to' dùng động từ nguyên thể ('decompose'). (Đáp án: decompose)
11
The industrial company was heavily fined for (discharge) untreated wastewater and chemical contaminants into the ocean.
💡 Giải thích: Sau giới từ 'for' (bị phạt vì hành vi gì), động từ chia ở dạng danh động từ 'discharging'. (Đáp án: discharging)
12
Every citizen can practice green living by (turn) off unnecessary electrical appliances when leaving the house.
💡 Giải thích: Sau giới từ 'by' chỉ phương thức, động từ chia V-ing ('turning off'). (Đáp án: turning)
13
The cafe replaced plastic smoothie cups with a glass jar, which (help) eliminate unnecessary single-use waste.
💡 Giải thích: Đại từ quan hệ ', which' thay cho cả sự việc thay cốc của quán cà phê, động từ chia số ít 'helps'. (Đáp án: helps)
14
Dr. Ha dedicated her scientific career to (find) biodegradable packaging materials that decompose rapidly in soil.
💡 Giải thích: Cấu trúc 'dedicate sth to doing sth': 'to' là giới từ nên động từ đi sau chia V-ing ('finding'). (Đáp án: finding)
15
Properly building a compost pile prevents an organic fruit peel from (rot) in landfills and releasing methane gas.
💡 Giải thích: Cấu trúc 'prevent sth from doing sth': sau giới từ 'from' động từ chia V-ing ('rotting'). (Đáp án: rotting)
16
Our school canteen decided to ban plastic straws, which (encourage) students to bring their own reusable metal straws.
💡 Giải thích: Mệnh đề ', which' thay cho việc nhà trường cấm ống hút nhựa, động từ chia số ít 'encourages'. (Đáp án: encourages)
17
The residential complex committed to (switch) entirely to solar green energy before the beginning of the dry season.
💡 Giải thích: Cấu trúc 'commit to doing sth' (cam kết làm gì): 'to' là giới từ nên động từ chia V-ing ('switching'). (Đáp án: switching)
18
Local wildlife conservationists succeeded in (protect) wild animals from consuming toxic landfill litter.
💡 Giải thích: Sau giới từ 'in' trong 'succeed in', động từ chia V-ing ('protecting'). (Đáp án: protecting)
19
The municipal council opened a rapid electric bus route, which (make) public transport far more appealing to urban commuters.
💡 Giải thích: Mệnh đề ', which' bổ nghĩa cho toàn bộ sự việc mở tuyến xe buýt điện, động từ chia ngôi thứ ba số ít 'makes'. (Đáp án: makes)
20
Before (refill) your reusable water bottle at the communal filtered station, please ensure it has been thoroughly sanitized.
💡 Giải thích: Sau giới từ/liên từ rút gọn chỉ thời gian 'Before', động từ chia ở dạng danh động từ 'refilling'. (Đáp án: refilling)
