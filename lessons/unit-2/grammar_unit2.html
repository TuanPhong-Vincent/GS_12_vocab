LESSON 2 – ARTICLES (REVIEW & EXTENSION)

Indefinite articles: A and An
  • We use the indefinite articles a and an before singular, countable nouns when the listener does not know exactly which person or thing we are referring to (first mention). e.g. I want to visit a cultural exhibition.
  • We also use a / an when stating someone's job, religion, or nationality: She is an anthropologist; he is a craftsman.
Article Phonetic Rule Usage Note Example (Unit Context)
a Before words beginning with a consonant sound Includes vowels pronounced with /j/ or /w/ sounds He bought a handcrafted souvenir from a European vendor.
an Before words beginning with a vowel sound Includes silent 'h' or acronyms starting with vowel sounds It was an honor to meet an elderly artisan from an ethnic community.
⚠ WATCH OUT!
  • Always choose a or an based on the spoken sound, not the written letter!
    • a university (begins with consonant sound /j/), a one-day festival (/wʌn/).
    • an hour (silent 'h' /aʊər/), an honest person, an MP3 player (/em/).
Definite article: The (Rules and Special Extensions)
  • We use the definite article the before singular or plural nouns when both the speaker and listener know which specific entity is meant.
Category Specific Rule Example (Unit Context)
Unique Entities Things of which there is only one in the world or universe The sun shines brightly over the equator.
Second Mention Nouns that have already been introduced previously in the discourse A craftsman made a bronze drum. The drum was displayed in the cultural museum.
Musical Instruments When referring to playing or learning a musical instrument Linh is learning to play the Dan Bau; Peter plays the guitar.
Superlatives & Ordinals With superlative adjectives and ordinal numbers This is the most vibrant festival; Charles Lindbergh was the first person to fly solo.
Plural / Compound Countries Countries with 'Kingdom', 'States', 'Republic', or plural nouns the UK, the US, the Philippines, the Netherlands.
Oceans, Seas, Mountain Ranges Names of oceans, seas, rivers, canals, deserts, and mountain chains The Pacific, the Atlantic, the Alps, the Mekong River.
⚠ WATCH OUT!
  • Do NOT use 'the' with single mountains or single lakes: Mount Everest (NOT the Mount Everest), Lake Baikal (NOT the Lake Baikal).
  • Do NOT use 'the' with most individual country names, continents, or cities: Viet Nam, Japan, Asia, Paris (NOT the Viet Nam).
Zero article (Ø) / No article
  • We do not use an article before plural countable nouns or uncountable nouns when speaking about them in a broad, general sense.
Classification Grammar Rule Example
General Plural Nouns Plural countable nouns referring to a whole class or group Ø Tigers are endangered animals; Ø museums preserve cultural heritage.
General Uncountable Nouns Abstract concepts, materials, or substances in general We cherish Ø peace, Ø mutual respect, and Ø cultural diversity.
Meals, Sports & Languages Routine meals, games/sports, and languages (without the word 'language') They enjoyed Ø lunch together, played Ø badminton, and spoke Ø English.
⚠ WATCH OUT!
  • Contrast general vs. specific use:
    • General: 'I love Ø traditional cuisine.'
    • Specific: 'I love the traditional cuisine of Hue.' (Defined by the phrase 'of Hue').
  • Notice the language difference:
    • 'She speaks Ø Japanese.' BUT: 'She speaks the Japanese language.'

Sentence Combination

1
• The annual carnival features a captivating blend of traditional music.
• It attracts thousands of international spectators every year.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use a relative clause with 'which'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: The annual carnival features a captivating blend of traditional music, which attracts thousands of international spectators every year.
Dùng đại từ quan hệ 'which' tạo mệnh đề quan hệ bổ nghĩa cho mệnh đề hoặc danh từ phía trước.
2
• The local chef used an organic secret ingredient.
• The speciality dish became the highlight of the food festival.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'Because'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: Because the local chef used an organic secret ingredient, the speciality dish became the highlight of the food festival.
Dùng liên từ 'Because' chỉ nguyên nhân kết quả, kết hợp chính xác mạo từ 'an organic secret ingredient' và 'the speciality dish'.
3
• Globalisation expands international economic trade.
• It also promotes cultural diversity among modern youth.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'Not only... but also'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: Not only does globalisation expand international economic trade, but it also promotes cultural diversity among modern youth.
Dùng cấu trúc đảo ngữ với 'Not only does... but it also...' hoặc cấu trúc song hành nối hai vị ngữ.
4
• Students learn more about unfamiliar customs.
• They appreciate cultural diversity more deeply.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'The more... the more'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: The more students learn about unfamiliar customs, the more deeply they appreciate cultural diversity.
Cấu trúc so sánh kép 'The more + S + V, the more + S + V' thể hiện mối tương quan tăng tiến.
5
• The young musicians formed a multicultural ensemble.
• They wanted to overcome language barriers through their art.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'in order to'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: The young musicians formed a multicultural ensemble in order to overcome language barriers through their art.
Dùng cụm chỉ mục đích 'in order to + V' để nối hai câu có chung chủ ngữ.
6
• The ancient citadel is a renowned tourist attraction.
• It was recognised as a World Cultural Heritage Site by UNESCO.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'which'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: The ancient citadel, which is a renowned tourist attraction, was recognised as a World Cultural Heritage Site by UNESCO.
Sử dụng mệnh đề quan hệ không xác định với 'which' để bổ sung thông tin cho chủ ngữ 'The ancient citadel'.
7
• Children dressed in creative Halloween costumes.
• They eagerly went trick or treating across the neighbourhood.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'Dressed in'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: Dressed in creative Halloween costumes, the children eagerly went trick or treating across the neighbourhood.
Rút gọn mệnh đề đồng chủ ngữ thành cụm quá khứ phân từ 'Dressed in creative Halloween costumes'.
8
• The exchange student experienced severe culture shock at first.
• She quickly adapted to the multicultural lifestyle in the United Kingdom.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'Although'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: Although the exchange student experienced severe culture shock at first, she quickly adapted to the multicultural lifestyle in the United Kingdom.
Dùng liên từ nhượng bộ 'Although' nối hai mệnh đề có ý nghĩa tương phản.
9
• Playing a traditional game like tug of war builds physical endurance.
• It also reinforces teamwork during school festivities.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'Besides'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: Besides building physical endurance, playing a traditional game like tug of war reinforces teamwork during school festivities.
Dùng 'Besides + V-ing' để bổ sung thêm lợi ích của hoạt động trò chơi dân gian.
10

• It allows young people to appreciate cultural differences effortlessly.
Liên từ gợi ý: Use 'By connecting'
→
💡 Câu kết hợp mẫu: By connecting individuals from diverse backgrounds, the internet allows young people to appreciate cultural differences effortlessly.
Dùng giới từ 'By + V-ing' diễn đạt phương tiện / cách thức mà 'the internet' tạo điều kiện cho giới trẻ.
