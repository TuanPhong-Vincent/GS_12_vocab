# Grammar Roadmap (Global Success 10–12)

Use this roadmap to confirm which grammar structures are appropriate for the target grade and unit. When generating grammar lessons, exercises, sentences, questions, options, and cloze passages, keep strictly within or below the target unit — do NOT borrow structures from higher units or higher grades (students haven't studied them yet).

If a current-unit structure would make a sentence feel forced or awkward, prioritize natural English phrasing.

> **Note on Lower Secondary Knowledge (Grades 6–9)**: All core grammar structures taught in lower secondary (GS6–GS9) — including basic tenses (present simple/continuous, past simple/continuous, future simple), basic modal verbs (can/could, should, must), comparative/superlative forms, simple/compound sentences, basic relative clauses, and basic conditionals — are considered foundational prior knowledge and are available for use in Grades 10–12.

## Global Success 10 (GS10)

- **Unit 1** (Family Life): Present simple vs. present continuous
- **Unit 2** (Humans and the Environment): The future with will and be going to, Passive voice
- **Unit 3** (Music): Compound sentences, To-infinitives and bare infinitives
- **Unit 4** (For a Better Community): Past simple vs. past continuous with when and while
- **Unit 5** (Inventions): Present perfect, Gerunds and to-infinitives
- **Unit 6** (Gender Equality): Passive voice with modals
- **Unit 7** (Viet Nam and International Organisations): Comparative and superlative adjectives
- **Unit 8** (New Ways to Learn): Relative clauses: defining and non-defining relative clauses with who, that, which, and whose
- **Unit 9** (Protecting the Environment): Reported speech
- **Unit 10** (Ecotourism): Conditional sentences Type 1 and Type 2
